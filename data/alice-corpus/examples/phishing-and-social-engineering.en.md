# Fake support and phishing

## Questions covered

- How does a fake support contact try to steal my funds?

## Short answer

Phishing and social engineering try to get a person to reveal a secret or authorize an action by deceiving them. The attack exploits their trust rather than a weakness in Bitcoin's cryptography.

## Explanation

A message can imitate a support team, announce an emergency or offer a fake update. The channels include emails, SMS, calls, social networks and imitation sites. The targets can be the recovery phrase, the password, an authentication code or remote access.

The right question is how the identity of the other party is established. Replying to the same message or following its link is not an independent verification.

## Limits and confusions

A unique password and two-factor authentication reduce some risks without making every exchange safe. A code can also be requested fraudulently. A support team does not need to receive the recovery words to explain how a wallet works. A reassuring, error-free message can nonetheless be malicious.
