# The double coincidence of wants

## Questions covered

- The double coincidence of wants: what is the mechanism, and what are its limits?

## Short answer

A barter exchange assumes that each party wants what the other is offering. A more widely accepted medium of exchange makes it possible to separate the sale of one good from the purchase of another.

## Explanation

- The problem is about needs having to meet at the same time.
- An intermediary of exchange reduces this constraint.
- Historical theories of the origin of money remain distinct from this teaching model.

## Limits and confusions

This model explains a difficulty of direct exchange; it does not prove that every society historically moved from barter to money.
