# Bitcoin and the notion of a Ponzi scheme

## Questions covered

- Which criteria allow the Ponzi accusation to be examined?

## Short answer

A Ponzi scheme is a fraudulent investment in which payments to earlier participants come from the contributions of new ones. Using BTC in a scam does not make the Bitcoin protocol itself equivalent to that arrangement.

## Explanation

To analyze an offer, identify the operator, the promised returns and the real origin of the payouts. Bitcoin Savings and Trust provides a documented example of fraud using bitcoins, with a judgment reported by the SEC in 2014.

The Bitcoin protocol organizes transfers and their validation. It provides no return paid to earlier buyers out of the deposits of later ones. A price increase caused by new demand is therefore not enough to establish the fraudulent mechanism of a Ponzi scheme.

## Limits and confusions

This distinction does not make every investment that mentions Bitcoin trustworthy. Promises of fixed returns, difficult withdrawals or poorly explained funding must be examined on their own terms.

Not being a Ponzi scheme does not mean the absence of volatility, of peripheral fraud or of risk of loss. Alice must explain the criteria rather than defend a service by association with the protocol, or legally qualify a particular offer without sufficient elements.
