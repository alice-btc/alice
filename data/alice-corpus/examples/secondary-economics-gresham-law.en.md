# Gresham's law and its conditions

## Questions covered

- Gresham's law and its conditions: what is the mechanism, and what are its limits?

## Short answer

Gresham's law sums up the phenomenon whereby, under legal tender at an imposed rate, bad money drives good money out of circulation.

## Explanation

- Users spend the degraded money and keep the better-quality money.
- The phenomenon appears in Roman reforms, devalued currencies and poorly fixed bimetallic systems.
- It generally assumes a legal or official ratio that prevents relative prices from adjusting freely.

## Limits and confusions

The formula assumes conditions such as an imposed parity between currencies. It does not mean that the money judged inferior wins in every free competition.
