# The mining cost is not a price floor

## Questions covered

- Must bitcoin necessarily be worth more than its production cost?

## Short answer

The mining cost indicates the profitability of a given operation, not a guaranteed price floor for bitcoin.

## Explanation

- When the price no longer covers an operation's costs, that operation can reduce or stop production.
- The difficulty adjustment then changes mining competition; it does not force BTC buyers to pay a minimum price.

## Limits and confusions

Costs and price can diverge; estimates from one group of miners do not represent the whole network.
