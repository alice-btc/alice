# Restrictions on future spending

## Questions covered

- What do the covenants proposed for Bitcoin allow?

## Short answer

Covenants restrict certain possibilities for the future spending of a UTXO. The constructions and guarantees depend on the mechanisms available or proposed.

## Explanation

- The owner accepts a spending rule that can limit the form or the destinations of later transactions.
- The uses discussed include protective vaults and congestion management; the different proposals do not offer the same capabilities.

## Limits and confusions

OP_CAT and OP_CTV must not be presented as activated without a dated verification.
