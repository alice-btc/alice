# Credit expansion and its interpretations

## Questions covered

- Credit expansion and its interpretations: what is the mechanism, and what are its limits?

## Short answer

Credit expansion refers to the increase in lending. When a bank grants a loan, it generally creates a deposit, subject to capital, liquidity and demand constraints. The Austrian analysis distinguishes this credit creation from financing out of prior savings and sees it as a possible source of cycles.

## Explanation

- According to the Austrian theory of cycles, credit financed by savings coordinates investment and future consumption better.
- In this analysis, a credit expansion disconnected from savings can distort interest-rate signals, encourage overinvestment and lead to its correction.
- This model does not establish that every credit expansion causes a bubble, nor that it alone explains every crisis.

## Limits and confusions

The creation of deposits through lending is a banking mechanism; the explanation of crises by Austrian theory must remain explicitly attributed.
