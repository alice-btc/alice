# Bitcoin, network and currency

## Questions covered

- What is the difference between Bitcoin and a bitcoin?

## Short answer

Bitcoin refers both to a payment network and to the asset exchanged on it. The network enforces common rules; bitcoins are the units whose spending conditions transactions change.

## Explanation

The distinction is similar to that between a settlement infrastructure and the money it allows to be transferred. Nodes verify transactions and blocks; wallets help their users receive and authorize spends. The price of 1 BTC, expressed in euros, is a matter of trading on markets.

The workings of the network, its uses and the value attributed to the asset can therefore be studied separately. A price drop does not, by itself, mean that transactions have stopped working.

## Limits and confusions

The distinction does not make these 2 aspects independent. Economic incentives contribute to the network's security. Technical usefulness guarantees no price and no return. A balance displayed by a platform may also represent a claim on that platform rather than bitcoins controlled directly.
