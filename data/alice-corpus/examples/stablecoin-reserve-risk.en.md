# Stablecoin reserves

## Questions covered

- What promises support a reserve-backed stablecoin?

## Short answer

For a reserve-backed stablecoin, the central question is which assets exist, where they are held and under what conditions they can fund conversion requests.

## Explanation

The quality and liquidity of the reserves matter as much as their stated value. Cash, bank deposits, securities or loans cannot be mobilized in the same way. Banks and custodians also add dependencies.

The segregation of assets, the issuer's commitments and the holders' rights have to be examined. Who can request a direct conversion? Under what conditions and with what delays? A holder may sometimes depend mainly on the secondary market rather than on direct access to redemption.

## Limits and confusions

A one-off attestation does not necessarily cover the same scope as a full audit. The date, the methods and the commitments included have to be made explicit. A statement of reserves does not guarantee their immediate availability in every crisis.

This framework does not give the current balance sheet of any particular issuer. For a concrete comparison, its most recent documents are the place to look, keeping its own statements distinct from independent verification. A price close to the target does not on its own prove the soundness of the backing.
