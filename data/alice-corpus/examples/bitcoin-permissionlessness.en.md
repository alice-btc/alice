# A network without prior permission

## Questions covered

- Do you need permission to use Bitcoin?

## Short answer

Bitcoin allows participation without obtaining authorization from a central authority of the protocol. A user can create keys, receive and propose transactions that respect the rules.

## Explanation

This openness concerns several roles. Developers can propose software; nodes can verify the ledger; miners can try to produce valid blocks. Nobody needs to create an account with a Bitcoin administrator to obtain an address.

This distinguishes the network from many services around it. An exchange may require registration and supporting documents. Those conditions belong to how it operates, not to the protocol's key creation mechanism.

## Limits and confusions

The absence of central authorization removes neither costs, nor access barriers, nor applicable laws. It does not guarantee that a miner will immediately include a payment, that a service will accept a customer or that a wallet will offer every feature. Access to Bitcoin provided by an intermediary can be far more restrictive than the network itself.
