# Comparing proof of stake and proof of work

## Questions covered

- Comparing proof of stake and proof of work — what is the mechanism and what are its limits?

## Short answer

Proof of stake groups together protocols in which participation in producing or validating blocks depends, among other things, on a locked-up asset. Bitcoin uses proof of work, whose resources and security assumptions differ.

## Explanation

- The locked-up asset plays a role in selecting or weighting participants.
- Some systems provide penalties for behaviors defined by their rules.
- The comparison with proof of work concerns costs, incentives and consensus conditions.

## Limits and confusions

Mechanisms, penalties and guarantees vary from one protocol to another. Compare a specific system rather than attributing identical security to every form of proof of stake.
