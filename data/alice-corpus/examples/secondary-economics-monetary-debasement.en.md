# Altering the metal content of money

## Questions covered

- Altering the metal content of money: what is the mechanism, and what are its limits?

## Short answer

Monetary debasement consists of reducing the quantity or quality of precious metal in a currency while maintaining its nominal value.

## Explanation

- It creates a gap between legal value and real value.
- It often triggers hoarding of the good coins and circulation of the bad ones.
- It can lead to rising prices, loss of confidence and disruption of economic calculation.

## Limits and confusions

Altering the metal content is not synonymous with an exchange-rate devaluation or an automatic, proportional rise in prices.
