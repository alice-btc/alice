# The free banking model

## Questions covered

- The free banking model: what is the mechanism, and what are its limits?

## Short answer

Free banking refers to a system in which competing banks issue means of payment under common rules, without a central bank's monopoly on issuance. Its supporters expect discipline to be exerted by customers and by the other banks.

## Explanation

- The possibility of conversion and trust in the issuer play a central role.
- Free banking models and their historical experiments are not all identical.
- Competition between banks and Bitcoin's issuance rules are distinct mechanisms.

## Limits and confusions

The benefits of competition are a thesis defended by this school of thought. They are not a guarantee against panics, fraud or liquidity failures.
