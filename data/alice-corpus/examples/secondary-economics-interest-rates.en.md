# Interest rates and their interpretations

## Questions covered

- Interest rates and their interpretations: what is the mechanism, and what are its limits?

## Short answer

An interest rate expresses the cost of credit or the agreed return on a loan over a period of time. It depends notably on risk, inflation expectations, market conditions and monetary policy.

## Explanation

- The same figure does not mean the same thing depending on the term and the conditions of the contract.
- Austrian analysis studies how rates coordinate saving and investment.
- Its critiques of artificially low rates are an interpretation of cycles, not proof that every rate cut causes a crisis.

## Limits and confusions

Distinguish nominal rate from real rate, credit risk and term. The interpretation of rates as coordinating savings belongs notably to Austrian analysis.
