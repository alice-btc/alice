# The emergence of money in Austrian analysis

## Questions covered

- The emergence of money in Austrian analysis: what is the mechanism, and what are its limits?

## Short answer

In the Austrian perspective, money emerges spontaneously from the market as the most saleable good, in order to overcome the limits of barter and the double coincidence of wants.

## Explanation

- Money does not need to be invented by the state in order to emerge.
- Actors converge on the most saleable and most widely accepted goods.
- Imitation and network effects reinforce monetary adoption.
- Bitcoin can be analyzed as an attempt at digital monetary emergence.

## Limits and confusions

The Austrian perspective presented here is a theory of monetary emergence; it does not replace the historical study of debts, institutions and practices.
