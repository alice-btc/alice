# The peg a stablecoin aims for

## Questions covered

- The peg a stablecoin aims for — what is the mechanism and what are its limits?

## Short answer

A stablecoin's peg is the intended anchor to a reference asset, for example 1 USD. It depends on redemption, reserves, arbitrage and confidence.

## Explanation

- A peg can break temporarily or permanently.
- Market depth and redemption capacity support the peg.
- The type of collateral determines the nature of the risk.
- A peg that holds in normal times does not prove resilience in a crisis.

## Limits and confusions

A price close to parity at a given moment proves neither the quality of the reserves nor the ability of each holder to redeem directly.
