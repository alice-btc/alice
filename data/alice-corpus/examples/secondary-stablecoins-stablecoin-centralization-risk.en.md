# The power of a stablecoin issuer

## Questions covered

- The power of a stablecoin issuer — what is the mechanism and what are its limits?

## Short answer

Stablecoin centralization risk comes from the issuer, the bank reserves, the smart contracts, the blacklist functions, the exchanges and the legal framework.

## Explanation

- A centralized stablecoin can be frozen or censored.
- The user depends on the issuer for redemption and peg maintenance.
- Compliance can come into tension with censorship resistance.
- This risk is the main difference from Bitcoin.

## Limits and confusions

Freezing powers and redemption conditions vary with the token and its governance. Keeping the peg does not erase the censorship risk.
