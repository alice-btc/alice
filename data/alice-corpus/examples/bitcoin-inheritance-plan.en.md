# Preparing the handover

## Questions covered

- How to think about inheritance without exposing your keys?

## Short answer

Preparing the handover consists of making the funds identifiable and recoverable by the authorized people if their holder dies or becomes unable to act. Understanding the procedure matters as much as the secrets that are kept.

## Explanation

Relatives must be able to recognize the tools, understand where to find the necessary information and distinguish a trustworthy contact from fake support. They will not necessarily be familiar with recovery words, the passphrase or multisig.

The documentation can describe the arrangement without exposing all the secrets to anyone who reads it. It must remain consistent after a change of wallet, of home or of the people involved.

## Limits and confusions

A technically possible procedure does not, on its own, settle succession and legal questions. This note determines no inheritance right. A complex arrangement perfectly memorized by its creator can become unusable for their relatives. Instructions meant for understanding must be distinguished from information that immediately allows spending.
