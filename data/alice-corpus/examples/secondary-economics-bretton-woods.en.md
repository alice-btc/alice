# The Bretton Woods system

## Questions covered

- The Bretton Woods system: what is the mechanism, and what are its limits?

## Short answer

After 1944, Bretton Woods organized a system in which the dollar was convertible into gold for central banks and the other currencies were pegged to the dollar.

## Explanation

- It gave the United States a central international monetary role.
- The system depended on American discipline in maintaining convertibility and sufficient reserves.
- Tensions from deficits and money issuance led to the Nixon Shock.

## Limits and confusions

Gold convertibility applied to foreign monetary authorities, not as an exchange guarantee for each individual. The parities were not immutable.
