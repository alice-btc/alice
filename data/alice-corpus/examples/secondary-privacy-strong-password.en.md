# Password strength and uniqueness

## Questions covered

- Password strength and uniqueness — what is the mechanism and what are its limits?

## Short answer

A strong password is long, unique, random and independent of personal information. Length alone is not enough if the pattern is predictable.

## Explanation

- Using a password manager's generator for ordinary accounts.
- Avoiding dates, names, dictionary words and predictable variations.
- Accommodating the absurd limits of some sites without reusing a pattern.
- The master password deserves particular attention and can be a strong passphrase.

## Limits and confusions

A long but predictable phrase remains vulnerable. The strength of the secret does not protect against entering it on a fake site or its capture by malicious software.
