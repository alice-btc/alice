# Merged mining

## Questions covered

- Can the same proof of work serve several chains?

## Short answer

Merged mining allows the same proof-of-work search to take part in validating a compatible auxiliary chain.

## Explanation

- An auxiliary chain accepts a construction that lets it reuse proof of work performed in another mining process.
- The auxiliary chain's security depends on the miners who actually take part and on that chain's own rules.

## Limits and confusions

The auxiliary chain does not automatically receive all of Bitcoin's guarantees or all of its hashrate.
