# Reading the idea of the white paper

## Questions covered

- What problem does the white paper try to solve?

## Short answer

The white paper presents a peer-to-peer electronic payment system. Its central problem is preventing double spending without asking a single institution to keep the accounts.

## Explanation

Published under the name Satoshi Nakamoto on 31 October 2008, this text describes the use of signatures, a transaction history and proof of work. Participants can verify payments and compare competing histories according to accumulated work.

The idea is to replace the role of a central arbiter with shared verification rules and incentives. This explains why blocks and confirmations matter for a payment. The text sets out the principle of the system; it is not the detailed manual of all later versions of Bitcoin.

## Limits and confusions

Political interpretations of Bitcoin are not all technical conclusions of the white paper. The white paper is not enough to explain how wallets or Lightning work today. It should not be credited with a demonstration of the 21 million cap, which comes from the issuance mechanism as implemented.
