# Passkeys and their recovery

## Questions covered

- Passkeys and their recovery — what is the mechanism and what are its limits?

## Short answer

Passkeys replace the password with a pair of cryptographic keys, often unlocked locally by biometrics or a PIN. The service does not receive the private secret.

## Explanation

- They reduce phishing and password reuse.
- They depend on the provider's availability, on the ecosystem and on device recovery.
- They can be simpler for the general public but require a backup strategy.
- They are part of a post-password trend.

## Limits and confusions

Recovery and synchronization depend on the device and on the provider. An authentication passkey does not replace a Bitcoin private key.
