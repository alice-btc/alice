# Behind-the-meter mining

## Questions covered

- What does powering a miner behind the electricity meter mean?

## Short answer

A behind-the-meter installation produces or consumes energy locally, upstream of certain metered exchanges with the grid.

## Explanation

- Local production can power consumption on the same site rather than passing entirely through the public grid.
- The economic outcome depends on the equipment, the grid connection and the opportunity cost of the other uses of that energy.

## Limits and confusions

It can remain connected to the grid; savings, fees and taxes depend on the site and the jurisdiction.
