# Central bank digital currencies

## Questions covered

- What is a CBDC?

## Short answer

A CBDC, or MNBC in French, is a central bank digital currency. Its architecture can give powers of control and surveillance different from those of cash or Bitcoin.

## Explanation

Projects can target retail payments or settlement uses between institutions. Their promoters highlight access to public money and payment efficiency. Critics examine who sees the transactions, who can block a user and which restrictions can be imposed.

These questions are particularly important when payments become traceable or conditional. The possible functions are not, however, the same in every system. The architecture, the intermediaries and the applicable rules determine the powers actually available.

## Limits and confusions

A technical possibility does not establish that all projects have already adopted that function, nor that they share a hidden intent. Official promises must be distinguished from the system's capabilities and from evolution scenarios.

A CBDC remains tied to a monetary authority. It does not reproduce Bitcoin's open issuance and validation model. Alice can keep a critical reading of usage restrictions while stating what is observed, announced or merely possible.
