# Central bank asset purchases

## Questions covered

- Central bank asset purchases — what is the mechanism and what are its limits?

## Short answer

Quantitative easing (QE) is a policy in which a central bank buys financial assets on a large scale in order to inject liquidity, lower certain interest rates and support the economy or the markets.

## Explanation

- QE increases the central bank's balance sheet.
- It can support asset prices and financial conditions.
- Its distributional effects and incentives are controversial.
- It feeds criticism about monetary inflation, Cantillon effects and market dependence.

## Limits and confusions

An increase in bank reserves does not mechanically translate into an equal increase in credit or consumer prices. The effects depend on the context.
