# Held bitcoin and price exposure

## Questions covered

- What is the difference between owning keys and holding a claim?

## Short answer

"Paper Bitcoin" informally refers to exposure to bitcoin without direct control of its keys. An ETF, a derivative contract and a balance on an exchange are, however, different structures.

## Explanation

Some products actually hold BTC for their investors; others rely on contracts or claims. The holder of the product depends on its rules, its service providers and its exit terms. They cannot assume they can directly spend the underlying bitcoin.

The possibilities of lending, pledging or rehypothecation must be checked product by product. For example, the IBIT prospectus of 2025-07-31 in principle prohibits these uses of the assets, with an exception concerning trading credits. This dated rule is not an independent verification of all operations.

## Limits and confusions

It is incorrect to assume that every ETF creates unbacked exposure or systematically reuses the BTC. The critique of dependence on intermediaries remains relevant, but must be kept separate from unproven factual accusations.

The concern that financialization reduces direct use is an economic analysis. Finally, a paper wallet is a medium for keys; the word "paper" does not make it a financial claim.
