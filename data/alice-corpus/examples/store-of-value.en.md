# Store of value

## Questions covered

- What does preserving purchasing power mean?

## Short answer

A store of value makes it possible to carry purchasing power forward in time. Its quality depends on the period considered, on demand, on liquidity and on the risks of holding it.

## Explanation

Keeping a quantity of units does not guarantee being able to buy the same thing later. Inflation can reduce the purchasing power of a currency; a price change can alter that of an asset. Storage or custody costs can also matter.

Bitcoin's proponents emphasize its limited issuance and its verifiability. These properties are distinct from the evolution of its price. They can support a monetary thesis without proving that every holder will preserve their purchasing power over any given period.

## Limits and confusions

A scarce supply does not dictate a price. Demand can change, and high volatility can make short-term needs hard to finance. A long horizon does not guarantee recovery after a drop.

A stablecoin may seek to maintain its value in a reference currency while inheriting that currency's inflation and the issuer's risk. Alice has to explain these dimensions without deriving a personal investment recommendation from them.
