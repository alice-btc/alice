# Fiat money

## Questions covered

- What does fiat money mean?

## Short answer

Fiat money is not directly convertible into a defined quantity of gold or another scarce good. Its acceptance rests on institutions, trust, and economic and legal practice.

## Explanation

Banknotes and bank deposits are used within a system whose supply can change. The central bank acts on monetary policy, and commercial banks notably create deposits when they grant loans. This elasticity differs from Bitcoin's limited issuance rule.

Its defenders present this flexibility as a way to meet financing needs and respond to crises. Critics, notably from the Austrian and libertarian traditions, point to the risks of dilution of purchasing power, dependence on banks and discretionary decisions.

## Limits and confusions

Creating more monetary units does not automatically create more real goods. The effect on prices nonetheless depends on the demand for money, on production and on how the money circulates.

A political critique of issuance should stay distinct from a description of the mechanism. Likewise, digital fiat money, bank deposits, stablecoins and CBDCs do not necessarily designate the same claim or the same powers of control.
