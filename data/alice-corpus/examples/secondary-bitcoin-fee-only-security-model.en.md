# Miner revenue based on fees

## Questions covered

- What could happen when the block subsidy becomes negligible?

## Short answer

As the subsidy decreases, fees take a potentially larger share of miner revenue.

## Explanation

- The transition shifts the cost of security from holders diluted by the subsidy to the users who pay fees.
- The balance will depend on demand for block space, the BTC price and miner behavior.
- A mempool that pays little can create incentives to reorganize fee-rich blocks.
- The topic matters but remains highly uncertain on a 2140 horizon.

## Limits and confusions

The future level of fees, hashrate and security remains uncertain; no future profitability is guaranteed.
