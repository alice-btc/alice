# Ransomware and the limits of backups

## Questions covered

- Ransomware and the limits of backups — what is the mechanism and what are its limits?

## Short answer

Ransomware blocks or encrypts data in order to demand a ransom. An attack can also copy the data and threaten to publish it.

## Explanation

- Separate, tested backups limit the losses caused by encryption.
- Cloud synchronization can replicate deletions or encrypted files.
- Updates and limiting permissions reduce some risks.
- Restoration has to start from a clean environment.

## Limits and confusions

A backup helps restore the data, but does not undo its exfiltration or a threat of disclosure. Paying does not guarantee recovery.
