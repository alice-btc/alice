# The subjective theory of value

## Questions covered

- The subjective theory of value — what is the mechanism and what are its limits?

## Short answer

The subjective theory of value holds that economic value does not come objectively from labor or costs, but from the preferences and judgments of individuals. Prices emerge from exchanges between actors who value goods differently.

## Explanation

- Value is ordinal and contextual, not measurable like a physical quantity.
- Voluntary exchange is possible because each party values what it gives and receives differently.
- The marginalist revolution replaced objective theories of value.
- This approach helps in understanding the gradual monetization of Bitcoin.

## Limits and confusions

This is a theoretical framework about value. It establishes neither a minimum value nor a return for Bitcoin; demand can change.
