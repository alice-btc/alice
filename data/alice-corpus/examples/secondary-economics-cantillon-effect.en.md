# Money creation channels and the Cantillon effect

## Questions covered

- Money creation channels and the Cantillon effect: what is the mechanism, and what are its limits?

## Short answer

The Cantillon effect refers to a mechanism described by Richard Cantillon and later taken up notably by the Austrian school. New money enters through specific channels and can change spending and relative prices before it spreads. Its first recipients can buy before certain price adjustments.

## Explanation

- New money enters through specific channels.
- Austrian and bitcoiner critiques stress the possible advantage of the first recipients, notably those with access to credit or public spending.
- Relative prices can change, not only the general price level.
- The mechanism is not enough to prove a systematic rise in inequality; the beneficiaries and the net effect depend on the channels, on employment and on price and income adjustments.
- The effect feeds bitcoiner critiques of discretionary monetary policies.

## Limits and confusions

The existence of different entry channels does not make it possible to automatically deduce the winners, the losers or the net change in inequality from a given policy.
