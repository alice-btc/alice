# Pool payout methods

## Questions covered

- What is the difference between PPS, FPPS and PPLNS?

## Short answer

Pools distribute revenue using different methods, which spread variance, fees and the risk of non-payment differently.

## Explanation

- With PPS, the pool absorbs a large part of the variability tied to block discovery; with PPLNS, the payout depends more on the blocks actually found.
- Payout thresholds, fees and the pool's solvency should be assessed together with the announced method.

## Limits and confusions

Each pool's contracts, thresholds and fees matter as much as the acronym of the method.
