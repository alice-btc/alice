# The functions of money

## Questions covered

- What is money for?

## Short answer

The classic functions of money are to facilitate exchange, preserve purchasing power and express prices in a common unit. A single instrument can fulfill these functions to different degrees.

## Explanation

As a medium of exchange, money avoids having to find a person who wants exactly the good one is offering. As a store of value, it makes it possible to defer spending. As a unit of account, it helps compare prices and keep accounts.

These functions make it possible to describe Bitcoin without reducing it to its price. One person can receive it as payment, another can hold it, while a merchant keeps displaying prices in euros and converts at the time of sale. Uses are therefore not necessarily identical.

## Limits and confusions

These functions are not a guarantee of economic success. Volatility can make it hard to preserve purchasing power in the short term; the technical possibility of paying does not guarantee acceptance by every merchant.

A dollar-backed stablecoin generally uses an existing unit of account, with the risks of its issuer and its backing. Comparing it with Bitcoin requires looking at each function and at control of the funds separately.
