# Specialized mining machines

## Questions covered

- Why do people generally not mine with an ordinary computer?

## Short answer

An ASIC is a circuit designed for a specialized task. For Bitcoin, mining machines use ASICs optimized for the proof-of-work computations based on SHA-256.

## Explanation

This specialization allows far more computations per unit of electricity than a general-purpose computer for the same task. By extension, "ASIC" often refers to the complete machine, with its chips, its power supply and its cooling.

Comparisons between machines focus in particular on their computing power, their consumption and their energy efficiency. Noise, heat and operating conditions must also be taken into account. A computer capable of verifying Bitcoin does not need to be an ASIC; verifying the rules and taking part in the mining competition are distinct activities.

## Limits and confusions

"More efficient" does not mean that the machine consumes little in absolute terms, nor that it will be profitable. A facility can run many powerful machines. The price of electricity, the purchase cost and the difficulty change the economic result.

Specialization also creates dependencies on manufacturers and on supply. An old model can have educational or thermal value without being competitive on its mining revenue alone.
