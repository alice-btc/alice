# Physical security

## Questions covered

- Why does discretion matter too?

## Short answer

The security of bitcoin also depends on protecting people. Linking an identity, a physical address and presumed holdings can create an exposure that goes beyond computer-related risks.

## Explanation

Information comes from posts, purchases, deliveries, meetings or leaks of identification data. Combining it can give a third party an idea of a person's habits and funds, even if that idea is inaccurate.

Discretion and limiting the data shared therefore contribute to security. A custody arrangement should be thought through with the consequences for loved ones and the understanding of the procedures in mind, not only with cryptographic criteria.

## Limits and confusions

A good signing device does not remove the holder's physical vulnerability. Databases of public incidents also reflect the cases that received media coverage and the classification methods; they do not automatically give the real frequency of the risk. This note sets out a protection principle and does not claim to predict individual risk from the amount held alone.
