# Moral hazard and guarantees

## Questions covered

- Moral hazard and guarantees — what is the mechanism and what are its limits?

## Short answer

Moral hazard appears when an actor takes more risk because they do not bear the full negative consequences of their choices. Guarantees, bailouts and lenders of last resort can amplify it.

## Explanation

- Systematically rescuing certain actors changes their incentives.
- Banks may take more risk if they anticipate public support.
- Moral hazard socializes losses while sometimes privatizing gains.
- Bitcoin is often presented as a reaction to this bailout regime.

## Limits and confusions

A guarantee can change incentives without automatically leading to reckless behavior. The effect depends on the rules, on oversight and on the losses that remain borne by the actor.
