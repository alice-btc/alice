# Commodity money

## Questions covered

- Why have certain commodities served as money?

## Short answer

Commodity money uses a good as a medium of exchange. Its physical properties, its acceptance and its scarcity influence its monetary usefulness.

## Explanation

A good that is durable, transportable, divisible and verifiable is easier to use for paying than an object that degrades quickly or whose quality is hard to recognize. Precious metals offer several of these properties, but their transport and verification also have costs.

Acceptance matters as much as the material. Holding a rare object is not enough to make it money if nobody wants to receive it. Conversely, easier production or a new source of supply can change its scarcity and its purchasing power.

## Limits and confusions

Narratives that take every society through a single sequence from barter to commodity money simplify a diverse history. This note describes a model of use, not a universal origin of money.

The comparison with Bitcoin concerns properties such as divisibility or verifiability; Bitcoin is not a physical material with industrial uses comparable to those of gold. Scarcity never guarantees, on its own, a market value.
