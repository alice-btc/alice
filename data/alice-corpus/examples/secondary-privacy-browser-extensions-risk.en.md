# The risks of browser extensions

## Questions covered

- What data can an extension expose?

## Short answer

Extensions can access pages and data according to their permissions; a compromised extension can therefore expose accounts or payments.

## Explanation

- Permissions can allow reading or modifying certain pages, or even access to sensitive data.
- A useful extension is still a software dependency to reassess after an update, a change of owner or unusual behavior.

## Limits and confusions

Limiting extensions and their rights does not mean that every password manager or autofill tool is to be avoided.
