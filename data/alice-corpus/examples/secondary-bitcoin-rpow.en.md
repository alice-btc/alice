# Hal Finney's reusable proofs of work

## Questions covered

- What was the principle of RPOW before Bitcoin?

## Short answer

Hal Finney designed RPOW to make proofs of work reusable; the system retained trusted components.

## Explanation

- RPOW belongs to the line of research on turning a proof of work into a reusable digital object.
- Its trust arrangement differs from the Bitcoin network, which coordinates spending through a chain validated by nodes.

## Limits and confusions

This precedent did not by itself offer Bitcoin's decentralized consensus.
