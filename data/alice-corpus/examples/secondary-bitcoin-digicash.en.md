# DigiCash before Bitcoin

## Questions covered

- Why did DigiCash's ecash still depend on intermediaries?

## Short answer

DigiCash commercialized David Chaum's ecash with partner issuers, before Bitcoin.

## Explanation

- The system combined blind signature techniques with a model built on partner issuers.
- This architecture shows that a currency can have strong privacy while still depending on intermediaries.

## Limits and confusions

Cryptographic privacy did not remove the dependence on the issuer and on the company.
