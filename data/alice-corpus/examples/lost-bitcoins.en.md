# Lost bitcoins

## Questions covered

- What happens to bitcoins whose keys are lost?

## Short answer

Bitcoins are considered lost when nobody has the means needed to spend them anymore. They are not automatically redistributed and do not change the issuance schedule.

## Explanation

The bitcoins remain recorded in the ledger, but their spending conditions can no longer be satisfied by their holder. This can result from the loss of the keys and all their backups, or from a lock that makes spending impossible.

Losing a phone or a signing device therefore does not necessarily mean losing one's funds. A compatible, complete and accessible backup can make it possible to recover the keys. The backup must also account for any passphrase and for the information the wallet needs.

## Limits and confusions

An address that has been inactive for a long time is not proof of loss. It can belong to someone who is deliberately holding their bitcoins. The total lost therefore cannot be measured precisely from inactivity. Losing a device, losing a copy of a key and being permanently unable to satisfy the spending conditions must be distinguished.
