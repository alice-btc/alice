# Hashrate

## Questions covered

- What does hashrate measure?

## Short answer

Hashrate measures a rate of hashing attempts per second. It describes the computing power committed to the search for proofs of work by a machine, a group or the network.

## Explanation

At a given difficulty, more attempts increase the probability of finding a block within a given time. This does not guarantee when the next success will happen. To compare a miner's share, the power of its competitors also has to be taken into account.

The global hashrate is not directly counted by a central meter. Estimates notably use the difficulty and the observed pace of blocks. An estimate over a short period can therefore fluctuate partly because of chance.

## Limits and confusions

Hashrate is neither electricity consumption nor revenue. Different hardware does not use the same energy to produce the same number of attempts. Costs and profitability require other data. A price change does not cause an instant mechanical change in the available hashrate either.
