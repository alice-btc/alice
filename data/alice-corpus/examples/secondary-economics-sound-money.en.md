# What sound money means

## Questions covered

- What sound money means — what is the mechanism and what are its limits?

## Short answer

Sound money refers to money that is hard to devalue, reliable over time and able to preserve economic calculation and savings.

## Explanation

- Historically, it is associated with coins of reliable weight and purity, then with gold.
- It stands in contrast to clipped, debased or over-issued currencies.
- In Bitcoin discourse, it refers to money that is scarce, verifiable and resistant to manipulation.

## Limits and confusions

"Sound money" is a normative concept whose criteria need to be made explicit. A supply that is hard to increase does not guarantee a stable price or constant purchasing power.
