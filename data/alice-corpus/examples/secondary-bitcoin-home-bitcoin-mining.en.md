# Home mining

## Questions covered

- Which constraints should be understood before mining at home?

## Short answer

Mining at home can serve to learn or to reuse heat, with constraints around noise, power consumption and safety.

## Explanation

- The purchase cost, electricity, ventilation, noise and uptime all play into the economics of home equipment.
- Recovered heat only has replacement value when a real heating need exists.

## Limits and confusions

An educational activity is not necessarily profitable, nor preferable to buying bitcoin.
