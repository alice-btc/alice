# Opportunity cost

## Questions covered

- Opportunity cost — what is the mechanism and what are its limits?

## Short answer

Opportunity cost is the value of the best alternative given up when a choice is made. It reminds us that every economic decision involves giving something up, even without a visible monetary expense.

## Explanation

- The relevant cost depends on the alternatives subjectively available.
- It applies to consumption, investment, production and the allocation of time.
- It is essential for understanding capital trade-offs and investment choices.
- In Bitcoin, it appears in holding, spending, mining, security and portfolio allocation.

## Limits and confusions

The relevant alternative must actually be available. For mining, temporarily surplus energy and energy that could be used elsewhere do not have the same opportunity cost.
