# Encrypting sensitive files

## Questions covered

- Encrypting sensitive files — what is the mechanism and what are its limits?

## Short answer

File encryption selectively protects certain sensitive documents or folders, independently of full disk encryption. It is useful for sharing, archiving or storing data at risk.

## Explanation

- It complements full disk encryption.
- It suits archives, identity documents, cloud backups and transmitted files.
- Security depends on the passphrase and on the tool chosen.
- Unencrypted temporary copies must not be left lying around.

## Limits and confusions

An encrypted file can leave plain-text copies in temporary files or backups. Encryption does not replace a recoverable backup.
