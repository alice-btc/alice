# Fractional reserve banking

## Questions covered

- How does a bank deposit differ from banknotes held directly?

## Short answer

Banks do not keep every deposit as immediately available money. They also hold loans and other assets, which creates a gap between withdrawal promises and their liquidity.

## Explanation

In the modern system, granting a loan generally creates a deposit. The bank does not simply take a pre-existing sum and lend it to someone else. This creation remains constrained by its capital, its funding, its liquidity, regulation, profitability and the demand for credit.

Deposits that can be withdrawn quickly thus finance assets whose repayment can be much slower. If many depositors ask for their money at the same time, the bank can run into a liquidity problem. A solvency problem, when its assets no longer cover its liabilities, is a separate question.

## Limits and confusions

The mechanism does not boil down to a fixed multiplier applied to reserves. Rules and practices differ between banking systems.

Deposit insurance and the lender of last resort address some risks, according to their conditions. Critiques of credit expansion should be kept distinct from the accounting description. A claim on a bank is not equivalent to bitcoin directly controlled by its keys.
