# The historical clipping of coins

## Questions covered

- The historical clipping of coins: what is the mechanism, and what are its limits?

## Short answer

Coin clipping consists of removing part of the precious metal while trying to keep the coin's face value.

## Explanation

- It is a historical form of devaluation and monetary fraud.
- It shows the difference between face value and real content.
- Authorities as well as individuals can have an incentive to tamper with money.
- Bitcoin makes the supply verifiable through consensus rules rather than through physical weight.

## Limits and confusions

Physical clipping, the official alteration of metal content and a fall in the exchange rate are different mechanisms; comparing them must not blur them together.
