# HTTPS and domain verification

## Questions covered

- HTTPS and domain verification — what is the mechanism and what are its limits?

## Short answer

HTTPS verification consists of checking the protocol, the exact domain, the padlock or certificate and the spelling of the URL before entering sensitive data.

## Explanation

- HTTPS encrypts transport but does not by itself prove that the site is legitimate.
- Attacks often use look-alike domains, deceptive characters or unusual extensions.
- The certificate has to match the expected domain.
- For critical accounts, email links are not followed; the URL is typed in or bookmarked.

## Limits and confusions

A valid certificate can belong to a fraudulent site. Checking the expected domain remains necessary; the icon displayed depends on the browser.
