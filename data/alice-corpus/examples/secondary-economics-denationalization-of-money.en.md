# The currency competition proposed by Hayek

## Questions covered

- The currency competition proposed by Hayek: what is the mechanism, and what are its limits?

## Short answer

Hayek proposed competition between private currencies in order to subject their issuers to the choice of users. This proposal is often compared with Bitcoin, whose issuance however follows protocol rules.

## Explanation

- Supporters of this competition expect it to discipline issuers.
- That expectation rests on assumptions about choice, competition and trust.
- Bitcoin has no issuer able to steer its supply on its own the way a private bank would.

## Limits and confusions

This is a theoretical proposal by Hayek. Its expected effects are not guaranteed results; Bitcoin differs from private currencies managed by competing issuers.
