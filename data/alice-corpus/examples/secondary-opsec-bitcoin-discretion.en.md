# Discretion about your bitcoins

## Questions covered

- Why avoid publishing your holdings and your storage habits?

## Short answer

Bitcoin discretion consists of limiting what you reveal publicly about your holdings, wallets, habits, addresses, storage locations and security practices. It protects both digital privacy and physical security.

## Explanation

- Talking publicly about amounts held increases social and physical risk.
- Photos, talks, social networks and informal conversations can create exploitable signals.
- Discretion does not prevent education; it separates teaching from personal exposure.
- It complements KYC minimization, OPSEC and physical security.

## Limits and confusions

Discretion reduces some exposure without eliminating physical risks or traces that are already public.
