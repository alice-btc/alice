# Bitcoin circular economies

## Questions covered

- What does earning and respending sats within a local community mean?

## Short answer

A Bitcoin circular economy makes it possible to earn and then respend sats with other participants, instead of systematically converting to another currency.

## Explanation

- The same income in sats can circulate between customers, workers and merchants if each of them finds a local use for it.
- The ability to spend depends on prices, equipment and actual acceptance, beyond the number of businesses advertised.

## Limits and confusions

The density of merchants, needs and liquidity determine how real this loop is.
