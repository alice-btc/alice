# The unit of account

## Questions covered

- Why display prices in a common unit?

## Short answer

The unit of account is the measure in which prices, debts and accounts are expressed. It can be different from the asset used to pay.

## Explanation

A merchant can display 1 price in euros, compute its equivalent in sats at the time of the sale, then receive bitcoin. In this example, the euro remains the unit of account and Bitcoin is used for settlement. Conversely, setting a price directly in sats means using the satoshi as the measure of the price.

A common unit makes comparisons between goods and accounting calculations easier. Its adoption depends on habits, contracts and the economic environment. The ability to make a payment in a currency is not enough to generalize prices in that currency.

## Limits and confusions

The volatility of the conversion rate can complicate displaying or holding a price. The moment of calculation and the validity period of a payment request have to be specified.

The fact that many prices are expressed in another unit does not sum up all of Bitcoin's monetary functions. Its uses as a medium of exchange or a store of value have to be examined separately, without assuming an automatic future trajectory.
