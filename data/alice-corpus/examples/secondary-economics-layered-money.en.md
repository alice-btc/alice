# Layers of monetary claims

## Questions covered

- Layers of monetary claims: what is the mechanism, and what are its limits?

## Short answer

Layered money describes a system in which a base layer (gold, reserves or central bank money) supports upper layers of banknotes, deposits and claims.

## Explanation

- In some historical convertibility systems, monetary claims could rest on other claims, themselves convertible into metal.
- Each layer adds usefulness and liquidity, but also counterparty risk.
- Modern fiat keeps layers, but without a final metallic anchor.

## Limits and confusions

Monetary layers built on claims are not the same as Bitcoin payment protocols. Each layer must be examined according to its settlement and custody mechanisms.
