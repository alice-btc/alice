# The 3-2-1 backup method

## Questions covered

- How does the 3-2-1 rule adapt to Bitcoin data and secrets?

## Short answer

The 3-2-1 method proposes several copies on different media, including 1 off-site, to limit correlated losses.

## Explanation

- Copies kept at the same site remain exposed to a shared disaster; diversification reduces this correlated risk.
- Restoration needs to be tested, and copies containing secrets protected against unauthorized access.

## Limits and confusions

For Bitcoin keys, multiplying copies also increases exposure; the model has to be adapted, and a seed is never sent in plain text to the cloud.
