# Bitcoin and stablecoins

## Questions covered

- Why is a dollar stablecoin not bitcoin?

## Short answer

Bitcoin and stablecoins do not offer the same guarantees. Bitcoin follows an issuance without a central issuer and its price varies; a stablecoin generally tries to track a reference unit, with a backing or stabilization mechanism.

## Explanation

For a stablecoin backed by a fiat currency, the goal is to limit variations relative to that unit. The reserves, the conversion rights and the role of the issuer then need to be examined. Keeping a value close to the dollar does not remove the variation in the dollar's purchasing power.

With Bitcoin in self-custody, the holder can control their keys without holding a claim on a BTC issuer. They remain exposed to custody errors and to market volatility. A Bitcoin balance left on a platform reintroduces an intermediary.

## Limits and confusions

Not all stablecoins work the same way. A description of fiat reserves must not be applied automatically to another mechanism. The stability aimed for is never synonymous with the absence of risk.

The comparison must distinguish volatility, censorship, privacy, liquidity and recovery. It explains usage trade-offs without automatically recommending an asset or a personal allocation.
