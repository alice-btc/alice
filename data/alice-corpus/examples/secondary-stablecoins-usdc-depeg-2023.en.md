# The USDC depeg episode of 2023

## Questions covered

- The USDC depeg episode of 2023 — what is the mechanism and what are its limits?

## Short answer

In March 2023, USDC temporarily lost its peg after a reserve exposure to Silicon Valley Bank was revealed, before returning toward 1 dollar after public intervention.

## Explanation

- It shows that a fiat-backed stablecoin also depends on the traditional banking system.
- The market quickly prices in reserve risk.
- The FDIC/Fed intervention limited the loss in this case.
- A reputable stablecoin can still suffer a crisis of confidence.

## Limits and confusions

This is a historical episode from March 2023, not a description of current reserves. The return to the peg in this case does not guarantee the outcome of a future crisis.
