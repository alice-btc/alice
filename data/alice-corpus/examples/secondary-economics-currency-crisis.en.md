# Currency crises and how they differ

## Questions covered

- Currency crises and how they differ: what is the mechanism, and what are its limits?

## Short answer

A currency crisis occurs when a currency suffers a rapid loss of confidence, external value or purchasing power. It can range from a sharp devaluation to hyperinflation and currency substitution.

## Explanation

- It can result from deficits, debt, money issuance, political crisis or an external shock.
- Actors seek refuge in foreign currencies, real goods or more reliable assets.
- Confidence can break faster than it is rebuilt.
- Bitcoin is sometimes used or imagined as a partial exit in these contexts.

## Limits and confusions

An exchange-rate crisis is not necessarily a hyperinflation. Bitcoin and stablecoins introduce other risks; no protection of purchasing power is guaranteed.
