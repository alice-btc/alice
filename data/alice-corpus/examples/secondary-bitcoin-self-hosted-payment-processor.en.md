# Self-hosting your payment processing

## Questions covered

- What control and what responsibilities come with a self-hosted payment processor?

## Short answer

Self-hosting your payment processor gives more control over the handling of payment requests and the data.

## Explanation

- It reduces the risk of censorship or blocking by a provider.
- It requires maintenance, security, backups and monitoring.
- It can be connected to your own Bitcoin/Lightning node.
- It gives more control but increases operational responsibility.

## Limits and confusions

The user must handle maintenance, security and backups; the hosting provider and connectivity can still create dependencies.
