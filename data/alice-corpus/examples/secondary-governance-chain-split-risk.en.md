# The risk of divergence between chains

## Questions covered

- What happens when nodes apply incompatible rules?

## Short answer

Chain split risk appears when nodes or miners apply incompatible rules and extend different branches of the blockchain.

## Explanation

- A split can be caused by a bug, a poorly coordinated upgrade or a social disagreement.
- Users, exchanges and wallets may see misleading confirmations during the incident.
- The severity depends on hashrate, on the economy and on how long the divergence lasts.
- Preserving consensus harmony is a priority of Bitcoin upgrades.

## Limits and confusions

A temporary divergence does not necessarily imply the lasting creation of 2 assets.
