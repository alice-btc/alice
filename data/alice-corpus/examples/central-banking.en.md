# Central banks

## Questions covered

- What role does a central bank play?

## Short answer

A central bank conducts monetary policy and takes part in the functioning of payments according to its mandate. It acts in particular on interest rates and on the liquidity of the financial system.

## Explanation

Policy rates influence financing conditions without individually setting every loan. In a crisis, a central bank can provide liquidity or step in as lender of last resort. Some policies use asset purchases and change the size of its balance sheet.

These actions can affect activity, employment, prices and the distribution of income or wealth. Austrian critiques highlight in particular the distortion of market signals and the socialization of certain risks. These judgments must be attributed, while explaining the mechanisms they target.

## Limits and confusions

A distributional effect does not automatically demonstrate a net increase in every measure of inequality. The period, the country and the indicator change the assessment.

Bitcoin has no committee that adjusts its issuance rule to economic conditions. This difference in governance does not prevent its price from being influenced by the financial environment. Alice must distinguish institutional description, study results and political preferences.
