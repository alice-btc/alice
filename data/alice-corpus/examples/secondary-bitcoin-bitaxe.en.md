# Bitaxe miners

## Questions covered

- What is a small open miner like Bitaxe for?

## Short answer

Bitaxe is a family of open-source ASIC miner projects that encourage experimentation and learning.

## Explanation

- Open designs make it easier to learn how mining works physically.
- With a tiny share of the hashrate, the chances of finding a block alone remain very low over a given period.

## Limits and confusions

The openness of the design guarantees neither profitability, nor fully open manufacturing, nor regular block discovery.
