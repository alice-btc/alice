# Keeping or converting payments received

## Questions covered

- What are the consequences for a merchant who keeps or converts the BTC received?

## Short answer

A merchant can choose to keep the BTC received or to convert part of it into another currency to cover expenses.

## Explanation

- Revenue in BTC and expenses in another currency create exposure to the exchange rate.
- Keeping and converting can each be chosen according to cash-flow needs, with their own costs and dependencies.

## Limits and confusions

A quick conversion reduces future exchange-rate exposure without eliminating fees, counterparty risk or price moves during processing.
