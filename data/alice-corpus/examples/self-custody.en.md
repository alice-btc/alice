# Self-custody of keys

## Questions covered

- What does it mean to hold your own bitcoins?

## Short answer

Self-custody consists of controlling the means needed to spend your bitcoins without the authorization of a custodian. It gives autonomy and transfers the responsibility for backup and recovery.

## Explanation

In a simple setup, the holder controls the signing keys. In a more elaborate setup, several keys or conditions may be required. The useful question is who can authorize, prevent or recover the spend.

Keeping a backup, its compatibility with the wallet and its future availability matter as much as the day-to-day operation of the application. Passing funds on to loved ones is also a problem distinct from protection against theft.

## Limits and confusions

Owning your keys does not mean verifying the chain yourself or eliminating hardware or software risks. A more complex setup can reduce security if it becomes incomprehensible. Non-custodial services can also introduce dependencies depending on their protocol. The real conditions of exit and recovery have to be examined.
