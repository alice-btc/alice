# Changing the scale of a monetary unit

## Questions covered

- Changing the scale of a monetary unit — what is the mechanism and what are its limits?

## Short answer

Redenomination consists of replacing a currency with a new unit by removing zeros or changing the nominal scale. It can simplify prices but does not solve the monetary causes if policy remains unchanged.

## Explanation

- It often happens after high inflation.
- It improves accounting readability but not necessarily purchasing power.
- Without monetary discipline, the zeros can come back.
- Latin American cases illustrate the repetition and limits of this strategy.

## Limits and confusions

Changing the scale of prices and balances does not in itself increase their real purchasing power. A redenomination and an economic stabilization are 2 different operations.
