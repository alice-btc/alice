# Recognizing scams

## Questions covered

- Which signals should raise the alarm about a Bitcoin offer?

## Short answer

Scams often use Bitcoin as a pretext to obtain a payment, a secret or access. A promise of guaranteed gains, an artificial urgency or a request for a recovery phrase should raise the alarm.

## Explanation

The scenarios include fake support, fake applications, gifts conditional on a payment and investments presented as risk-free. The fraudster frequently tries to make the person act before they verify the identity and the context.

The presence of a known logo, a social media account or a polished website proves nothing on its own. A legitimate person can also have their account compromised. Verification must go through a channel independent of the message received.

## Limits and confusions

A scam using BTC does not demonstrate that the Bitcoin protocol is itself the fraudulent arrangement. Conversely, the security of the protocol does not make the offers that use its name trustworthy. Technical understanding does not remove the need for caution against human manipulation. This note offers no miraculous recovery method after a transfer.
