# Ready-to-install node solutions

## Questions covered

- What do distributions like Umbrel or RaspiBlitz simplify?

## Short answer

A node-in-a-box is a distribution or hardware solution that simplifies installing and administering a Bitcoin node and associated applications. Umbrel, Start9, RaspiBlitz, MyNode and RoninDojo illustrate this family.

## Explanation

- These solutions lower the technical barrier to running a node.
- They often add a graphical interface and an app store.
- They also introduce dependencies on their distribution, its security and its updates.
- They are useful for learning, but do not replace understanding the basics.

## Limits and confusions

Easy installation does not remove maintenance, backups and distribution dependencies.
