# Official or spontaneous dollarization

## Questions covered

- Official or spontaneous dollarization: what is the mechanism, and what are its limits?

## Short answer

Dollarization is the use of the US dollar, officially or informally, in place of or alongside a weakened local currency. It often appears after high inflation, a crisis of confidence or hyperinflation.

## Explanation

- It can stabilize prices by importing a more credible currency.
- It reduces the monetary sovereignty of the local state.
- It can be official, or spontaneous on the part of private actors.
- Bitcoin can be compared to an alternative form of voluntary currency substitution.

## Limits and confusions

Stability against the dollar does not guarantee constant purchasing power. Stablecoins add issuer, reserve, network and conversion risks.
