# Helping relatives through a Bitcoin inheritance

## Questions covered

- How can a family be helped without taking possession of its private keys?

## Short answer

Inheritance support can explain the principles and point relatives in the right direction without asking for private keys. Recovery must respect the rights of the heirs and the existing backup setup.

## Explanation

- Never ask for or receive private keys or hardware wallets at a meetup.
- The goal must stay educational: explaining Bitcoin, the risks and the next steps.
- The family should be directed to local legal rules on inheritance, gifts and conversion.
- A standard procedure reduces the risk of theft, abuse or unclear moral responsibility.

## Limits and confusions

Never hand secrets over to the support group; inheritance rules must be checked locally.
