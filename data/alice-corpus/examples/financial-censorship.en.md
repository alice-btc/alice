# Financial censorship

## Questions covered

- How can access to a means of payment be restricted?

## Short answer

Financial censorship refers to the blocking or restriction of access to funds and payments by an actor that controls a chokepoint. It can take the form of a frozen account, a refused withdrawal or a blocked payment.

## Explanation

A banking intermediary, an exchange or a digital issuer can hold different powers. One needs to identify where the decision is made and what the user can still do without its cooperation. Transaction surveillance can also increase the ability to target certain activities.

Bitcoin used with one's own keys reduces dependence on a custodian of the funds. This property explains its relevance for financial sovereignty. It does not make network access, key protection and the means of getting a payment accepted any less necessary.

## Limits and confusions

Resisting censorship does not mean that no transaction can be delayed or that no user comes under pressure. Exchanges, conversion services and some layers can reintroduce points of control.

Whether a restriction is justified or abusive is also a matter of law and political judgement. Alice should explain the concrete mechanism and its effects rather than assume that all restrictions look alike or that every digital instrument offers the same guarantees.
