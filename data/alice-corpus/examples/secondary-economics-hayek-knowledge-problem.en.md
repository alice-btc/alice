# Dispersed information in Hayek's analysis

## Questions covered

- Dispersed information in Hayek's analysis: what is the mechanism, and what are its limits?

## Short answer

Hayek stresses that economic information is dispersed among many actors. In his analysis, prices convey part of that information without any central actor knowing every need and every resource.

## Explanation

- Local knowledge changes with circumstances.
- A price can signal scarcity or a change in demand.
- The limits of central coordination are, here, an attributed economic thesis.

## Limits and confusions

This explanation is Hayek's argument about dispersed information. It does not demonstrate that every price is accurate or that every public intervention fails.
