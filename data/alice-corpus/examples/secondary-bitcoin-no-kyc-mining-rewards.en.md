# Receiving rewards without KYC

## Questions covered

- Does mining without identity checks make the income anonymous?

## Short answer

Depending on the pool, mining rewards can be received without going through a purchasing platform that requires KYC.

## Explanation

- Mined coins do not carry the history of an exchange purchase.
- Privacy depends on the pool, the payout address, the network and the amounts.
- Withdrawal thresholds and fees can force an accumulation at the pool.
- Do not confuse no-KYC with absolute anonymity.

## Limits and confusions

The pool, the payout addresses and network data can nevertheless allow correlations.
