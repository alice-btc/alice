# BTCPay invoices

## Questions covered

- What is the difference between a BTCPay invoice and a confirmed payment?

## Short answer

A BTCPay invoice is a payment request with an amount, an expiry and the accepted methods. It is used to track the customer's settlement.

## Explanation

- They define the amount, currency, expiry and available payment methods.
- They make it possible to track status and confirmation.
- They can be generated by the PoS, a crowdfund, the API or a payment request.
- Their life cycle is central to a merchant workflow.

## Limits and confusions

Displaying an invoice or a browser redirect does not prove final settlement; the status must be checked.
