# Finding merchants with BTC Map

## Questions covered

- How can you spot businesses that accept Bitcoin?

## Short answer

BTC Map helps locate places that accept Bitcoin payments, based on community contributions.

## Explanation

- The map is a starting point for finding merchants; it does not replace confirmation with the place itself.
- Community data can be outdated or incomplete, and the accepted payment methods can change.

## Limits and confusions

Being listed on the map does not guarantee that the business still accepts Bitcoin; confirm locally.
