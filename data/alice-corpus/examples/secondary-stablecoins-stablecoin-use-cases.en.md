# Possible uses of stablecoins

## Questions covered

- Possible uses of stablecoins — what is the mechanism and what are its limits?

## Short answer

Stablecoins can be used to transfer value denominated in a currency, to receive payments or to hold a unit of account temporarily. They retain the risks tied to their issuer or to their backing mechanism.

## Explanation

- An international transfer still depends on access to on-ramps and off-ramps.
- A digital dollar unit can make some payments easier without removing the exchange-rate risk against local expenses.
- Payments and treasury are possible uses, not investment recommendations.

## Limits and confusions

Possible uses prove neither measured adoption nor guaranteed protection. Costs, access to conversion, applicable law and freezing risk depend on the context.
