# Authenticating with LNURL-AUTH

## Questions covered

- How can a wallet replace a password for a compatible service?

## Short answer

LNURL-AUTH lets a compatible wallet answer an authentication request cryptographically, without a traditional password.

## Explanation

- It replaces some accounts with a cryptographic proof provided by the wallet.
- It reduces the need to store passwords and sensitive data.
- It can simplify purchases, subscriptions and access to services.
- It should be understood as a UX building block, not as a universal identity solution.

## Limits and confusions

It is not proof of legal identity, and losing the key data can affect access to the service.
