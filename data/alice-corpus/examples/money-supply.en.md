# The money supply

## Questions covered

- What does the quantity of money measure?

## Short answer

The money supply measures the quantity of money within a defined scope. It must not be confused with the quantity of banknotes alone or with all the wealth of an economy.

## Explanation

The aggregates can include cash, bank deposits and other forms of liquidity. Their definitions vary between institutions. Comparing figures therefore requires specifying the country, the period and the scope.

In the modern banking system, loans generally create deposits, under constraints of capital, liquidity and demand. Creating monetary units does not directly produce real goods. Their circulation can change relative prices and the distribution of purchasing power depending on the channels through which they enter.

## Limits and confusions

A rise in an aggregate does not mechanically cause an identical rise in all prices. Production, the demand for money and credit conditions also matter.

The supply of BTC verifiable by nodes comes from a different mechanism. Claims on bitcoin held by intermediaries do not create additional valid BTC on the chain. This distinction makes it possible to compare the systems without confusing the monetary base with financial promises.
