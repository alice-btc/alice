# Responding to data breach alerts

## Questions covered

- What to do when an account or identifier shows up in a breach?

## Short answer

Data breach monitoring consists of checking whether an email or identifier appears in compromised databases, then prioritizing password rotation and enabling additional protections.

## Explanation

- An email present in a breach increases the risk of targeted phishing.
- Reused passwords turn a minor breach into a chain of compromises.
- Breach alerts call for a password change and a review of active sessions.
- Monitoring does not replace prevention; unique passwords and 2FA remain essential.

## Limits and confusions

The absence of an alert does not prove the absence of a breach; a seed or private key is never submitted to a lookup service.
