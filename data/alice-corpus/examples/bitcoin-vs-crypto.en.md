# Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies

## Questions covered

- What are the differences between Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies?

## Short answer

The word "crypto" groups together very different systems. Bitcoin must be examined according to its own rules, its issuance and its validation method, without automatically attributing those properties to other projects.

## Explanation

Bitcoin uses proof of work and monetary rules verified by nodes. Other systems may depend on an issuer, a foundation, a reserve of assets or another consensus mechanism. A token issued on a platform is not necessarily a currency that works like Bitcoin.

To compare, the useful questions concern modification rights, issuance conditions, custody, verification and the possibilities of blocking. A name, a logo or the use of the word blockchain is not enough to answer them.

## Limits and confusions

The comparison does not allow classifying all other projects as identical or fraudulent. Nor does it erase the risks tied to Bitcoin or its intermediaries. Commercial promises and promises of returns must be distinguished from the demonstrable properties of the system. This note explains differences; it recommends no purchase.
