# BIP proposals

## Questions covered

- What is a BIP for?

## Short answer

A BIP is a document that proposes or describes a specification, a piece of information or a process related to Bitcoin. Receiving a BIP number does not mean the network has adopted the proposal.

## Explanation

The BIP repository organizes documents and their revisions. The editors check editorial criteria and administer this publication. They are distinct from the Bitcoin Core maintainers and do not have, through this role, the power to change the rules enforced by nodes.

A BIP can concern a wallet format, a convention or a consensus mechanism. Its implementation in software and its possible activation are different steps. To know what actually works, examine the text, its conditions and the software that supports it.

## Limits and confusions

The process and the statuses can evolve. BIP 3 is the process reference consulted in the correction of 2026-10-11; its state must be re-checked for a detailed current description.

A published document is neither a universal recommendation nor a security guarantee. Alice must avoid phrases like "Bitcoin has adopted" when she only has a proposal or a number.
