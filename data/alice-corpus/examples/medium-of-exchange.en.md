# Medium of exchange

## Questions covered

- What makes a currency usable for paying?

## Short answer

A medium of exchange is a good or an instrument accepted to settle purchases. It makes trade easier without requiring that each seller want exactly what the buyer could offer in return.

## Explanation

Its usefulness depends on acceptance, ease of transfer, divisibility and the cost of payments. Using Bitcoin can go through the chain or through systems such as Lightning, with different constraints on fees, availability and custody.

A merchant can accept a payment in bitcoin while calculating their price in another currency. The means of payment and the unit of account then remain distinct. Likewise, what is received can be kept in BTC or converted by a service, which changes the dependencies.

## Limits and confusions

The technical possibility of paying guarantees neither acceptance by a seller nor the availability of a Lightning route. Settlement, amount errors and refunds have to be organized.

Stablecoins often provide a way to transfer a digital fiat unit, but how they work depends on the backing and the issuer. Comparing media of exchange therefore requires looking at control of the funds and the actual conditions of the payment.
