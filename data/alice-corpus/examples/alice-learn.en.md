# Learning with Learn

## Questions covered

- What is the Learn space for?
- What is the difference between reading a course and asking Alice for an answer?

## Short answer

Learn is a library of courses and tutorials about Bitcoin. You can read the chapters, track your progress and take the available quizzes. Alice can explain a passage on request.

## Explanation

The content comes from Plan ₿ Academy, under the CC BY-SA 4.0 license. Reading the course and the explanation generated by the assistant are 2 distinct pieces of content.

The learning path lets you start a course, open its chapters and resume reading. Progress is saved locally when the application's storage is available. Some content offers links to Explorer to observe examples.

French and English are planned as content embedded in the application. Other languages may require a download from a remote source. The reading language is chosen independently of the conversation language.

## Limits and confusions

Not every course has a quiz. A chapter marked as read does not certify that it has been mastered. Downloading a language does not guarantee that every piece of content will remain accessible offline; availability depends on the files actually present and kept. Alice's explanations may contain errors.
