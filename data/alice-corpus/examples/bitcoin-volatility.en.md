# Bitcoin's volatility

## Questions covered

- Why can the price vary so much?

## Short answer

Volatility describes the magnitude of variations in Bitcoin's price over a period. It is distinct from its technical rules and can make its short-term purchasing power unpredictable.

## Explanation

Market liquidity, expectations, speculation and economic or regulatory shocks influence prices. The same quantity of BTC can therefore correspond to very different amounts in another currency.

Some bitcoiner readings interpret this volatility as a stage in the monetization of an emerging good. That is an economic interpretation, not proof that the price will converge toward a given value. A limited supply guarantees neither growing demand nor a future price.

## Limits and confusions

A long horizon does not ensure recovering from a drop. Leverage can amplify losses and trigger a liquidation before any later move.

Periodic buying of a fixed amount spreads out the purchase dates, without guaranteeing a profit or a better average price. Alice can explain this mechanism and the risks without prescribing an amount, a schedule or an allocation. A useful payment function must not be turned into a promise of financial performance.
