# The first merchant uses

## Questions covered

- What does Bitcoin Pizza Day represent?

## Short answer

Bitcoin Pizza Day commemorates the exchange of 10,000 BTC for 2 pizzas announced by Laszlo Hanyecz on 2010-05-22. Another user, jercos, arranged the delivery.

## Explanation

The original BitcoinTalk thread contains the proposal and then the confirmation of the exchange. The episode illustrates the move from a technical experiment to the purchase of a real good. It does not mean the pizzeria itself took bitcoins as payment.

The amount seems very high when a later price is applied retrospectively. That comparison does not describe the information or the possibilities available to the participants at the time. The historical interest lies mainly in putting a medium of exchange into practice and in the gradual formation of uses.

## Limits and confusions

The account documents a famous exchange; it does not prove that no earlier merchant transaction existed. It is therefore better to speak of one of the first documented uses than to claim absolute priority without proof.

This episode should not be used to make people feel guilty about spending their bitcoins, or to promise a future price increase. A currency used to pay fulfils a different function from its mere speculative holding.
