# Digital security habits

## Questions covered

- Which habits protect my accounts and devices day to day?

## Short answer

Digital hygiene is the set of simple, repeatable habits that reduce exposure to common attacks: unique passwords, 2FA, updates, backups, caution with links and separating uses.

## Explanation

- Seeking perfect security is unrealistic; the goal is to reduce the most likely risks.
- Progress works best in stages: secure email, password manager, 2FA, backups, browser and network.
- Tools do not replace behavior: checking the URL, the sender, where software comes from and unusual requests.
- For a bitcoiner, this foundation also protects exchange accounts, cloud access, public content and recovery procedures.

## Limits and confusions

A routine reduces frequent risks without guaranteeing that a device is clean.
