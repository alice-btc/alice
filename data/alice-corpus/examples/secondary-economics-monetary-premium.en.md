# The notion of monetary premium

## Questions covered

- The notion of monetary premium: what is the mechanism, and what are its limits?

## Short answer

In some analyses, the monetary premium refers to the value attributed to an asset for its uses as money or as a reserve, rather than for its non-monetary uses.

## Explanation

- Reserve demand can add to industrial or consumer demand.
- The distinction helps compare the uses of an asset, without directly measuring their separate value.
- Interpretations of this premium depend on the economic framework adopted.

## Limits and confusions

The monetary premium is an analytical lens that is hard to isolate empirically. On its own it does not give a numerical valuation of Bitcoin or gold.
