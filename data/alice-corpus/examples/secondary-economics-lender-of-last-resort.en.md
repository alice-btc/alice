# The lender of last resort

## Questions covered

- The lender of last resort: what is the mechanism, and what are its limits?

## Short answer

The lender of last resort is the institution, generally the central bank, that provides liquidity to banks or markets in crisis in order to avoid a systemic collapse. This role reduces some panics but can create moral hazard.

## Explanation

- It responds to the instability of fractional reserve banks.
- It can prevent cascades of failures.
- It sometimes encourages risk-taking if actors anticipate a rescue.
- Bitcoin's genesis block echoes the context of the bank bailouts.

## Limits and confusions

Providing liquidity and recapitalizing are different things. The existence of a lender of last resort does not guarantee the rescue of every bank or every creditor.
