# Funding Bitcoin Core development

## Questions covered

- Who funds the work of Bitcoin Core developers?

## Short answer

Bitcoin Core developers can be funded by grants, donations or independent companies. Funding does not grant the power to impose a new rule on the network.

## Explanation

- Bitcoin Core does not sell a classic proprietary piece of software.
- Developers can be funded by different organizations.
- Diversity of funding reduces some risks of capture.
- Sustainable funding remains a challenge for critical open-source software.

## Limits and confusions

Diverse backing can reduce some risks of dependence without guaranteeing the absence of influence.
