# Casascius physical coins

## Questions covered

- What does a physical coin associated with bitcoins actually contain?

## Short answer

Casascius coins hid a private key under a hologram. Their value in bitcoin depends on the funds still controlled by that key.

## Explanation

- They gave virtual bitcoins a physical form as collectible objects.
- They made Bitcoin tangible for the public.
- They involve an initial custodial/trust model towards the manufacturer.

## Limits and confusions

The physical object proves neither that the key is secret nor that the funds are still there.
