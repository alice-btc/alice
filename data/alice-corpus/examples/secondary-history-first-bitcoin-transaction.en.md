# The first known transfer between users

## Questions covered

- To whom did Satoshi send the first known bitcoins?

## Short answer

The first known Bitcoin transaction between 2 people took place on 2009-01-12, when Satoshi Nakamoto sent 10 BTC to Hal Finney. It marks Bitcoin's passage from launched software to a network in use.

## Explanation

- Hal Finney is the first known recipient of a Bitcoin transaction.
- The transaction concretely tested the peer-to-peer sending described in the whitepaper.
- It links Satoshi and Finney in the network's first days.
- It has become a major historical landmark.

## Limits and confusions

This is the first known transfer between people, distinct from the first coinbase transaction.
