# The utility of an additional unit

## Questions covered

- The utility of an additional unit: what is the mechanism, and what are its limits?

## Short answer

Marginal utility refers to the value attributed to an additional unit of a good according to the actor's preferences and circumstances. It explains the formation of demand and the marginalist revolution in the theory of value.

## Explanation

- The value of a unit depends on its marginal use, not on an abstract total value.
- It resolves several classical paradoxes of value.
- It supports the subjective theory of value.
- It helps analyze monetary demand and Bitcoin's monetary premium.

## Limits and confusions

Utility depends on the actor and the context; this notion does not provide a formula for deducing Bitcoin's future price.
