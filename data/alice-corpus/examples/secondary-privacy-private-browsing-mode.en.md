# The limits of private browsing

## Questions covered

- The limits of private browsing — what is the mechanism and what are its limits?

## Short answer

Private mode mainly limits the local traces of a session: history, persistent cookies and forms. It does not make you anonymous to the ISP, the site visited or a corporate network.

## Explanation

- Useful for temporary sessions, one-off searches or avoiding certain persistent cookies.
- It does not hide the IP address or network activity.
- It does not protect against malware, phishing or fake sites.
- It is best explained as a tool for reducing local traces, not as Tor.

## Limits and confusions

Private mode does not hide the IP address. Downloaded files and the traces kept by services or the network do not disappear when the window is closed.
