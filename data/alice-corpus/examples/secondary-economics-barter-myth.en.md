# The debate over the historical barter narrative

## Questions covered

- The debate over the historical barter narrative: what is the mechanism, and what are its limits?

## Short answer

The barter myth is the anthropological critique of the idea that primitive societies first relied heavily on direct barter before inventing money.

## Explanation

- David Graeber argues that primitive societies work mainly through credit, gifts, debt and social obligation.
- This critique does not deny the existence of barter between strangers or distinct groups.
- It calls for distinguishing primitive societies, the great ancient civilizations and international trade.

## Limits and confusions

The critique targets a universal historical narrative of barter. It denies neither its existence nor the usefulness of money for facilitating exchanges; the positions must remain attributed.
