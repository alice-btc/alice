# The metadata hidden in files

## Questions covered

- What information can a photo or a document reveal?

## Short answer

Metadata hygiene consists of identifying and removing the information hidden in files, photos, documents or communications: location, device, author, dates, software and editing history.

## Explanation

- Photos can contain GPS coordinates, the device model and timestamps.
- Documents can reveal the author, organization, local paths or history.
- Cleaning metadata matters before publishing or sensitive sharing.
- Removal has to be verified, not assumed.

## Limits and confusions

Erasing metadata does not remove the clues visible in the content itself or the copies already distributed.
