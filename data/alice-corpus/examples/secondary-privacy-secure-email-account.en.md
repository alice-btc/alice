# The role of email in account recovery

## Questions covered

- The role of email in account recovery — what is the mechanism and what are its limits?

## Short answer

The email address is often used to recover other accounts. Its compromise can therefore give access to several services, even if their passwords are different.

## Explanation

- Using a unique password and protecting the recovery methods.
- Enabling an additional factor supported by the service.
- Reviewing open sessions and authorized access.
- Distinguishing TOTP codes from phishing-resistant security keys.

## Limits and confusions

TOTP remains vulnerable to real-time phishing. A weak recovery method can bypass strong authentication.
