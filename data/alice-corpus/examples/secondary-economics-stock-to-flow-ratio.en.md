# The stock-to-flow ratio and its limits

## Questions covered

- The stock-to-flow ratio and its limits — what is the mechanism and what are its limits?

## Short answer

The stock-to-flow ratio compares the existing stock of a good to its annual production, and measures how hard it is to dilute the existing supply.

## Explanation

- Gold keeps a high ratio because it is accumulated and little consumed.
- Metals that are rarer in annual production can be less monetary if their stock is small or quickly consumed.
- The ratio helps in understanding monetary scarcity, store of value and competition between monetary goods.

## Limits and confusions

This relationship between stock and flow does not account for all of demand and is not a law of price formation. It cannot guarantee a future price for Bitcoin.
