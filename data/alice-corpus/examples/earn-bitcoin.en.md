# Receiving bitcoin for work

## Questions covered

- Can one obtain bitcoin without buying it?

## Short answer

Receiving bitcoin for work or a sale makes it possible to obtain some without first buying it on an exchange. This assumes a clear agreement on the price and the payment terms.

## Explanation

The parties need to distinguish the unit in which the price is set from the asset that is paid. A service can be invoiced in a local currency and then settled in BTC at the agreed rate, or be priced directly in sats. The moment of conversion and the validity of the payment request must be understood on both sides.

The choice between receiving on-chain, over Lightning or through an intermediary service changes the fees, the custody and the experience. Once received, the payment can be kept or converted; receiving it does not remove the risks tied to that decision.

## Limits and confusions

The cited course sometimes presents this use as a more organic form of adoption than speculative buying. That is an assessment of the use, not a guarantee of lower risk or additional income.

Accounting and tax obligations depend on the country, the activity and the date. This note explains the general mechanism without giving a universal legal rule. Volatility remains significant between setting the price, receiving the funds and using them later.
