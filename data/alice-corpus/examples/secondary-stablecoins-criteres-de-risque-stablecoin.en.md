# Reading the risks and evidence of a stablecoin

## Questions covered

- Reading the risks and evidence of a stablecoin — what is the mechanism and what are its limits?

## Short answer

Stablecoin risk criteria make it possible to evaluate a stablecoin beyond its yield or popularity: peg maintenance, reserve quality, transparency, audits, liquidity, redemption mechanisms, integrations, legal framework and censorship risk. The criteria vary depending on whether the stablecoin is fiat-backed, crypto-collateralized or algorithmic.

## Explanation

- For a fiat-backed stablecoin, look at reserves, liquidity, account segregation, audits and redemption capacity.
- For a crypto-backed stablecoin, look at overcollateralization, liquidations, oracle risk, governance and market depth.
- Centralization can improve regulatory clarity but increases the risk of freezing or censorship.
- A stablecoin has to be evaluated according to its use: trading, DeFi, treasury, payments, arbitrage or dollar exposure.

## Limits and confusions

An attestation at a given date, a reserve report and a full audit do not have the same scope. No single criterion guarantees the peg or access to redemption.
