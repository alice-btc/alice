# The preference between present and future

## Questions covered

- The preference between present and future — what is the mechanism and what are its limits?

## Short answer

Time preference measures the preference for present satisfaction over future satisfaction. In Austrian economics, it underpins the originary rate of interest and influences saving, investment, capital and decision horizons.

## Explanation

- A lower time preference favors saving and long-term investments.
- A higher time preference favors immediate consumption.
- Interest rates coordinate decisions between present and future.
- Hard money is often associated with a drop in time preference in bitcoiner narratives.

## Limits and confusions

Time preference does not sum up every constraint on saving. Associating Bitcoin with a drop in this preference is an interpretation, not a demonstrated automatic effect.
