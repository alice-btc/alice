# Claims convertible into metal

## Questions covered

- Claims convertible into metal: what is the mechanism, and what are its limits?

## Short answer

A banknote or certificate convertible into metal makes it possible to transfer a claim rather than carry gold or silver. Its holder then depends on the custodian's ability to honor the conversion.

## Explanation

- Portability improves, but an intermediary keeps the metal.
- Full backing must be distinguished from issuance exceeding the reserves.
- A restriction or suspension of conversion changes the nature of the promise.

## Limits and confusions

A promise of conversion depends on the contract and on the issuer's reserves. Do not generalize this mechanism to the origin of all paper currencies.
