# Price discovery

## Questions covered

- How does a market price form?

## Short answer

Price discovery results from offers, demands and trades. It gathers dispersed information without guaranteeing that the resulting price is fair, stable or impossible to manipulate.

## Explanation

Participants do not necessarily assign the same value to a good and do not have the same liquidity needs. Their decisions make transaction prices emerge. Market depth and access to information influence how new demand translates into the price.

The Austrian school emphasizes subjective valuations and the signals transmitted by trades. This reading grid helps understand some bitcoiner arguments, but must be attributed when it becomes a general interpretation of the economy.

## Limits and confusions

An observable price is neither a certain measure of intrinsic value nor a forecast of the next price. Access constraints, manipulation or thin liquidity can degrade the quality of the signal.

The production cost of a bitcoin does not automatically set its price. A buyer is not obliged to cover a miner's expenses. Describing price discovery therefore does not make it possible to recommend an entry point or announce a guaranteed floor.
