# The noise of mining machines

## Questions covered

- Can the noise of an ASIC be reduced without compromising its cooling?

## Short answer

Industrial ASICs can produce significant noise. Reducing this noise means preserving the cooling and taking the power draw into account.

## Explanation

- The noise comes notably from the fans needed to remove a lot of heat from a small volume.
- Slowing the fans without adapting heat dissipation can expose the hardware and the installation to overheating.

## Limits and confusions

Do not modify fans or enclosures without checking the manufacturer's limits and electrical safety.
