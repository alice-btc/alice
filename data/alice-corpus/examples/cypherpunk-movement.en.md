# The cypherpunk origins

## Questions covered

- Which ideas came before Bitcoin?

## Short answer

The cypherpunks defend privacy and autonomy through cryptographic tools. Bitcoin belongs to this history of research into protected communications and digital money.

## Explanation

The movement brought together programmers, cryptographers and activists around discussions, writings and software. Their approach was to build usable protections rather than rely solely on institutional promises.

Work on ecash, proof of work and digital currencies came before Bitcoin. Projects such as Hashcash, b-money or Bit Gold shed light on this context without being identical to the system Satoshi proposed. The Bitcoin network combines cryptographic mechanisms and incentives to organize a transaction history without a single central authority.

## Limits and confusions

This lineage does not mean that every participant shared the same opinions, or that each invention came from a common organization. Technical contributions, public exchanges and political interpretations need to be kept apart.

Knowing a precursor does not make it possible to identify Satoshi. Similarities in interests or skills are weak clues, not proof. The educational value lies in understanding the problems these researchers were trying to solve, notably surveillance and dependence on intermediaries.
