# Mises's regression theorem

## Questions covered

- Mises's regression theorem: what is the mechanism, and what are its limits?

## Short answer

Mises's regression theorem explains that the current monetary demand for a good is rooted in an earlier exchange value, going back to an initial non-monetary value. It is used to explain the origin of the value of money.

## Explanation

- According to Mises, money does not arise arbitrarily without a history of value.
- The debate around Bitcoin concerns the nature of its initial value: collectible, curiosity, production cost, network or censorship-resistant utility.
- The theorem is often discussed in Austrian analyses of Bitcoin.
- On its own it is not enough to settle the whole theory of Bitcoin's monetization.

## Limits and confusions

Present this reasoning as a theory of Mises and its application to Bitcoin as a debate; it is neither a proof of price nor a technical condition of the protocol.
