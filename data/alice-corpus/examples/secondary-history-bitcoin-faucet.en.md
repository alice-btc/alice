# Gavin Andresen's historic faucet

## Questions covered

- Why were bitcoins given away for free in the network's early days?

## Short answer

Gavin Andresen's Bitcoin Faucet gave away bitcoins for free to encourage experimentation and attract new users. At the time, BTC had little value and the main goal was bootstrapping the network.

## Explanation

- The faucet made Bitcoin testable without mining or buying.
- It helped widen the community of early users.
- It illustrates a time when giving away several BTC for free was rational for adoption.
- In hindsight, it has become a symbol of how bitcoin's value has evolved.

## Limits and confusions

The historic faucet distributed real BTC; the playground's test sats are of a different nature.
