# The debt theory of money

## Questions covered

- The debt theory of money: what is the mechanism, and what are its limits?

## Short answer

The debt theory of money holds that money emerges from relationships of debt, credit and quantified social obligations rather than from direct barter.

## Explanation

- It is associated with Alfred Mitchell-Innes and David Graeber.
- It sheds light on Mesopotamian tablets, social debts and administrative units of account.
- Its strong version treats all money as a debt or claim on society, a thesis contested by the example of commodity money.

## Limits and confusions

This theory is one reading of the origins and nature of money. It does not demonstrate that every current form of money is a debt owed by an issuer.
