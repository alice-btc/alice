# The costs of mining

## Questions covered

- What does a miner's profitability depend on?

## Short answer

Mining is profitable only if its revenue covers all of its costs. A calculator gives a scenario based on assumptions, not a guaranteed future income.

## Explanation

Revenue depends notably on hashrate, difficulty, the subsidy, block fees and the exchange rate used to convert the bitcoin received. Costs include electricity, the purchase and depreciation of hardware, its maintenance, pool fees and downtime.

Heat can have value if it replaces heating that is actually needed. That saving must be calculated separately and over a realistic period. Unused heat in summer is not thermal revenue. Comparing several assumptions for difficulty, uptime and electricity cost makes the calculation more informative than a single estimate.

## Limits and confusions

Revenue expressed in euros can vary even if the quantity of bitcoin received stays the same. Conversely, the quantity received can fall if competition increases.

The average production cost does not impose a price floor on bitcoin. Alice can explain these mechanisms and show a fictional example; she must not turn this calculation into personal advice on buying ASICs or on investments.
