# The suspension of dollar convertibility in 1971

## Questions covered

- The suspension of dollar convertibility in 1971 — what is the mechanism and what are its limits?

## Short answer

The Nixon Shock refers to the 1971 suspension of the dollar's convertibility into gold, which ended the Bretton Woods peg and accelerated the modern fiat era.

## Explanation

- Dollars could no longer be exchanged for gold by other countries.
- The event removed an important constraint on American monetary expansion.
- It marks a major break in the monetary history of the 20th century.
- It feeds Bitcoin analyses about non-manipulable money and limited supply.

## Limits and confusions

The 1971 suspension concerns the official international convertibility of the dollar into gold. The transformation of the monetary system extends beyond this single decision.
