# When long-dormant bitcoins move

## Questions covered

- Do old bitcoins moving necessarily signal a sale?

## Short answer

Bitcoins that move after a long period of inactivity may correspond to a sale, but also to a wallet migration, an inheritance or a simple reorganization of funds.

## Explanation

- An address waking up does not automatically mean a sale: it can be an internal transfer, a technical migration, a payment, an inheritance, a key recovery or profit-taking.
- Analysis must distinguish the destination, change, dust, exchange labels and the later path of the UTXOs.
- Very old BTC that move are often read as a market signal, but that reading remains probabilistic.

## Limits and confusions

Age and on-chain movement alone prove neither identity nor intent.
