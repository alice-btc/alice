# Open-source mining hardware

## Questions covered

- What can actually be inspected in an open-source miner?

## Short answer

Open-source mining hardware projects publish some board designs, software and procedures to make inspection and repair easier.

## Explanation

- Access to the board designs and firmware allows more people to inspect, adapt and repair the hardware.
- Chip manufacturing, supply and performance remain subject to industrial constraints.

## Limits and confusions

This does not mean that the ASIC chips or all the components are themselves open.
