# A Cypherpunk's Manifesto

## Questions covered

- Which conception of privacy did Eric Hughes defend?

## Short answer

Eric Hughes's manifesto defends privacy and the ability to choose which information is revealed, thanks to cryptography.

## Explanation

- The text stresses the ability of individuals to choose which information to reveal.
- Building software is presented as a way to make that privacy practical.

## Limits and confusions

The positions of a manifesto do not necessarily represent all Bitcoin users.
