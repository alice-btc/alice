# Tokens backed by a commodity

## Questions covered

- Tokens backed by a commodity — what is the mechanism and what are its limits?

## Short answer

A commodity-backed stablecoin is backed by a commodity such as gold, oil or a tokenized physical asset.

## Explanation

- It makes exposure to a physical asset more portable and divisible.
- It depends on a custodian and on redemption procedures.
- The token's liquidity can differ from that of the underlying asset.
- XAUt illustrates tokenized gold exposure.

## Limits and confusions

A token indexed to gold can vary strongly in dollar terms. Price exposure, ownership rights and the contractual possibility of delivery or redemption have to be distinguished.
