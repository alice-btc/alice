# Freshly mined bitcoins

## Questions covered

- What does the expression virgin bitcoin mean?

## Short answer

The expression virgin bitcoin generally refers to bitcoins that come directly from a block reward, with no prior spending history.

## Explanation

- A coinbase output is identifiable in the chain; later payouts may already add a transaction history to it.
- The idea of a purity premium comes from market practices and raises a fungibility debate; it is not a category of bitcoins created by consensus.

## Limits and confusions

A pool payout can come from an ordinary transaction; the absence of history means neither anonymity nor a guaranteed price premium.
