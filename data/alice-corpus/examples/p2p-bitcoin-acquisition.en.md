# Peer-to-peer trades

## Questions covered

- What does buying or selling peer-to-peer mean?

## Short answer

A P2P trade directly connects people who want to buy or sell bitcoin. It can reduce some dependencies on a central exchange, but does not remove the risks of the transaction.

## Explanation

Trades can use different payment methods and sometimes a matching or escrow service. One needs to understand who controls the funds at each step, how a dispute would be handled and which conditions trigger their release.

Privacy also depends on the payment method. A bank transfer, a conversation or a meeting can reveal information even without a centralized identity file. Fees and price spreads can pay for the service or reflect limited liquidity.

## Limits and confusions

P2P means neither anonymous nor free of intermediaries. An escrow can add a dependency, and a direct trade exposes one to fraud or to personal safety risks. A proof of payment presented by the other person is not always equivalent to a final settlement.

Alice should explain the mechanisms and the risks without promising the absence of traces or giving a legal rule valid everywhere. The availability and the conditions of a specific service must be verified separately.
