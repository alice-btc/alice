# The risks of cloud mining

## Questions covered

- What risks does a cloud mining offer hide?

## Short answer

Cloud mining exposes you to a provider that promises a share of hashrate or revenue without giving control of the hardware.

## Explanation

- The customer depends on the contracts, the reports and the provider's ability to distribute the announced revenue.
- Interruptions, real costs and termination clauses can make the return very different from the marketing projections.

## Limits and confusions

Profitability, fees, whether the machines really exist and the withdrawal conditions must be checked; a guaranteed return is a warning sign.
