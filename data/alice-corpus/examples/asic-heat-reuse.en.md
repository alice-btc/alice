# Heat recovery

## Questions covered

- Can the heat from the machines be used?

## Short answer

An ASIC turns almost all of its electricity into heat. This heat can be used for heating if it is captured in the right place and at the right time, in addition to the mining work performed.

## Explanation

The thermal gain comes from the heating that is replaced. If the home would have used an electric radiator, the heat from an ASIC can provide part of the same service. By contrast, producing heat in a room that is already too warm avoids no useful consumption and may require additional cooling.

The assessment must therefore specify the need, the season, the recovery losses, the price of electricity and the equipment replaced. Mining revenue can reduce the net cost, but varies with the difficulty, block fees and the bitcoin price. Noise, ventilation and the safety of the installation also matter in real use.

## Limits and confusions

A heat pump can deliver more useful heat per electric kWh than an ASIC alone. The same thermal service must be compared, not only the nominal power of the devices.

Recovering heat does not automatically cancel the energy footprint or the emissions. The benefit depends on the reference scenario and on the heat actually put to use over the year.
