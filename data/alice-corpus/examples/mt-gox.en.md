# The lesson of Mt. Gox

## Questions covered

- Why does the story of Mt. Gox remain important?

## Short answer

Mt. Gox was one of the first large bitcoin exchanges. Its collapse became a major example of the risk associated with keeping funds with an intermediary.

## Explanation

An exchange makes it easier for buyers and sellers to meet and contributes to market liquidity. When it also holds the assets, the balance visible to the customer depends on its infrastructure and on its ability to honor withdrawals.

The story of Mt. Gox shows that access to an account is not equivalent to direct control of Bitcoin keys. An exchange can stop making funds available while the protocol keeps working. Users then have to depend on the company's procedures and on the available remedies.

## Limits and confusions

An exchange incident is not automatically a flaw in the Bitcoin protocol. Conversely, the robustness of the protocol does not protect against the errors or fraud of a custodian.

This note gives the lasting custody lesson, without claiming to summarize the current state of repayments. Amounts, beneficiaries and timelines must be verified from dated documents before being discussed. Comparisons with other services should concern how they actually operate, not only their age or reputation.
