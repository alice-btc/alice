# Holding your keys under censorship

## Questions covered

- Is self-custody enough if the Internet or platforms are blocked?

## Short answer

Bitcoin self-custody under censorship refers to the ability to control your keys when platforms, banks, the Internet or states become hostile or unavailable.

## Explanation

- Centralized exchanges can be blocked, censored or become inaccessible.
- Self-custody protects against some platform risks but not against Internet shutdowns or physical attacks.
- The topic connects geopolitics, opsec, privacy and financial resilience.

## Limits and confusions

Self-custody guarantees neither a connection to the network nor the physical safety of the holder.
