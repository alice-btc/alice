# Algorithmic stablecoins

## Questions covered

- Algorithmic stablecoins — what is the mechanism and what are its limits?

## Short answer

A so-called algorithmic stablecoin aims at a peg through issuance rules, burning rules or market incentives rather than a simple full reserve in the target currency.

## Explanation

- The peg depends in particular on liquidity and on confidence in the mechanism.
- A drop in confidence can trigger chain reactions and a loss of the peg.
- The real collateral and the exit options need to be examined, not just the algorithmic label.

## Limits and confusions

The mechanisms differ between purely algorithmic, crypto-collateralized and hybrid models. An issuance rule does not guarantee the value on the market.
