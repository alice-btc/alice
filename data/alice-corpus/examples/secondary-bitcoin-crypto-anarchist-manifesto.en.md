# The Crypto Anarchist Manifesto

## Questions covered

- Which ideas did Timothy May associate with cryptography?

## Short answer

Timothy May's Crypto Anarchist Manifesto presents cryptography as a means of transforming the control of exchanges and communications.

## Explanation

- The text links the technical possibilities of cryptography to a transformation of power relations.
- It serves as intellectual context for the cypherpunks; this political program must be distinguished from Bitcoin's technical mechanisms.

## Limits and confusions

It is a historical political position, not a neutral description of guaranteed effects of the protocol.
