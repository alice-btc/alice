# The block size debate

## Questions covered

- Which disagreements drove the block size war?

## Short answer

The block size debate set several visions of Bitcoin's capacity and governance against each other. It was as much about the cost of payments as about the ability to verify the rules yourself.

## Explanation

Supporters of larger blocks emphasized fees and the adoption of on-chain payments. Advocates of more cautious limits highlighted the resources required by nodes and layered solutions. These positions should be attributed rather than presented as indisputable facts.

In 2017, the story includes the Bitcoin Cash split, the activation of SegWit and the abandonment of the planned SegWit2x hard fork. Its promoters announced its suspension on 2017-11-08, citing the lack of sufficient consensus. Stopping the timeline in August therefore leaves out an important step.

## Limits and confusions

It was not a simple vote in which 1 group held central authority. The software in use, node validation, mining and economic acceptance interacted.

Interpreting the outcome as a victory for autonomy or for caution is a governance reading. It must remain distinct from the dates, the changes activated and the proposals abandoned.
