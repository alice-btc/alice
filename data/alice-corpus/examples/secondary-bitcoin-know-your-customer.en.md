# KYC identity checks

## Questions covered

- Which information does a financial service ask for under KYC?

## Short answer

KYC covers the identity and customer-knowledge checks put in place by financial services.

## Explanation

- Identification can concern the person, the beneficial owners of a company, and the origin or purpose of the operations.
- Collecting documents creates a dependence on the provider's security and data-retention policy.

## Limits and confusions

The documents required and the obligations depend on the service and the jurisdiction.
