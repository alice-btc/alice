# Preparing the handover of your digital accounts

## Questions covered

- How can loved ones be given access without exposing secrets in advance?

## Short answer

A digital inheritance procedure lets authorized loved ones understand which accounts and data exist and how to go about recovering them.

## Explanation

- The password manager can centralize the inventory of accounts.
- A paper or notarized procedure can explain how to gain access without publishing the secrets.
- Access to data, financial accounts, Bitcoin and public accounts are distinguished.
- A trusted person's understanding of the procedure is tested.

## Limits and confusions

Planning for access does not automatically grant the legal right to use every account and must not expose the secrets prematurely.
