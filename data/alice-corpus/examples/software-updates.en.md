# Understanding updates

## Questions covered

- Why update your software?

## Short answer

Updates can fix vulnerabilities, bugs and compatibility problems. Leaving software exposed indefinitely with known vulnerabilities increases the risks.

## Explanation

Maintenance covers the system, the browser, applications, extensions and sometimes the firmware of devices. An update has to come from an authentic channel; a pop-up window or an alarming message is not proof of one.

For a wallet, the backup and recovery information has to remain known before any maintenance operation. Reading the relevant changes makes it possible to understand whether they affect compatibility or the data format.

## Limits and confusions

A new version is not infallible and not every change has the same urgency. The right approach depends on the software's context, its fixes and the project's verified guidance. Bitcoin Core updates do not automatically mean that the monetary rules change or that every user is forced to adopt them.
